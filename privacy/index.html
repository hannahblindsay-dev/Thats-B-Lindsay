Your privacy.

thatsblindsay is the studio of Hannah Lindsay, based in Cape Town, South Africa. This notice explains what happens to your information when you visit this site or get in touch. It is written to meet South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What this site collects

This site has no forms, no accounts and no analytics or advertising cookies. It does not collect your personal information directly.

Services this site uses

  • Hosting (GitHub Pages). The site is served by GitHub, which may log technical data such as your IP address for security and to keep the site running.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). Typefaces load from Google, which receives your IP address to deliver them.
  • Booking (TidyCal). If you book a call, TidyCal collects the details you enter, such as your name and email, so we can meet.
  • WhatsApp. If you message on WhatsApp, your number and messages are handled by WhatsApp (Meta) and by me to reply.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

How I use what you send me

Only to reply, to plan and deliver work you’ve asked about, and to keep basic business records. I don’t sell your information or add you to a mailing list without asking.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you, or object to how it’s used. Email hello@thatsblindsay.com and I’ll respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator or, in the EU, your local data protection authority.
