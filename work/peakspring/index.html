Water that nourishes from within.

PeakSpring sells a medically certified water ionisation system to research-driven buyers who are sceptical of anything that feels like a placebo. We built a brand they could trust.

The thinking.

(Certification and results should speak, not marketing copy)
  1. 01A category built on distrust.

    A high price point and a sceptical, research-driven buyer. Every brand value (Living Water, Purposeful pH, Conscious Footprint) translates a technical benefit into something felt.

    The brief
  2. 02Restraint as credibility.

    Two audiences, health and fitness buyers and parents, and one governing idea: resist over-explaining the science. Four logo concepts were scored against five criteria and benchmarked against Bang & Olufsen, Oura Ring and LARQ.

    The strategy
  3. 03Quiet luxury.

    Grounded, refined, assured: confident without being loud. Photography leans on natural light and honest textures, drawn from water and mountains, not glossy stock.

    Creative direction
“The result: water that nourishes from within.”
What makes PeakSpring different · Brand guidelines

Where the brand landed.

(Packaging, apparel, lifestyle, in use)

The brandbook carries the identity all the way to shelf and product: three bottle colourways, apparel, and the photography that puts it to work.

One brand, two reasons to buy.

(The campaign)

The audience splits into two clear segments. Rather than force one message onto both, we built two campaign directions, each speaking to what that customer actually cares about.

Direction 01Athletic & Performance

Built for people who sweat for it: heat, distance, and hydration that keeps up with both.

Direction 02Wellness & Purity

The brand softened into daily ritual: purity, presence, and the things worth slowing down for.

A segmented funnel built for a real sales team.

Athletes page+Parents page→One CTA→CRM & automation

The positioning became a Webflow funnel with distinct landing pages for each audience, both leading to a single call to action, plus CRM, automation, SEO, analytics and pixel tracking to support acquisition.

A drop with a story inside.

(The mark)

One confident shape that holds the whole brand: water, origin and growth. It was chosen from four concepts, each scored against five criteria and benchmarked against Bang & Olufsen, Oura Ring and LARQ.

  1. 01
    The drop

    A clean water-drop outline: the product reduced to its simplest, most ownable form.

  2. 02
    The P

    Inside, a single unbroken line draws a P for PeakSpring, flowing like water rather than sitting like a letter.

  3. 03
    The source

    At the heart of the P sits a smaller form, part droplet and part new leaf: Living Water, made visible.

  4. 04
    The stem

    The P’s stem rises from the base like a spring breaking ground, purity drawn up from the source.
