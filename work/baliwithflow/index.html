Where the real Bali begins.

A curated travel brand founded by Flora, a long-time Bali local. One brand had to hold a €40 self-guided guide, curated day trips and €15,000 VIP vacations, and then a live pivot, without ever feeling like a patchwork.

The thinking.

(One path, not three funnels)
  1. 01Three offers, one brand.

    Flora sells a self-guided PDF guide, curated day trips and high-ticket VIP vacations. The challenge was letting the cheapest and the priciest offers live under one brand without either undercutting the other’s credibility.

    The brief
  2. 02Built to hold a pivot.

    Persona research across luxury travellers, digital nomads and eco-conscious couples shaped one customer journey holding all three price tiers. When the business grew to include small-group escapes mid-engagement, the architecture simply extended.

    The strategy
  3. 03Quiet luxury with soul.

    Calm, authentic and grounded in nature, avoiding both budget-travel cliché and cold corporate luxury. The tone treats a €40 guide and a €15,000 trip as two doors into the same considered world.

    Creative direction
“Travel becomes more than movement. It becomes awakening.”
Company description · BaliwithFlow styleguide

The identity, off screen.

(Studio door to travel bag)

The BaliwithFlow mark carried off screen, from the studio door to the bag it travels in.

Three offers, still handled by one person.

Figma→Webflow+Neurodesign+CRM and payments→One system

The positioning was carried into a Figma-to-Webflow build with neurodesign and animation applied to the conversion path, plus CRM and payment automation connecting the guide, day trips and high-ticket inquiries into one system, and a foundational SEO and tracking setup.

One line, with flow.

(The mark)

The B of Bali, drawn as one soft, looping stroke: a letter that reads as a path, which is exactly what the brand sells.

  1. 01
    The B of Bali

    The initial carries the whole name, so the mark works on its own on a tote, a card back or a favicon.

  2. 02
    A path, not a letter

    The stroke enters from outside the letter and loops through it, like a route leading in: the flow in BaliwithFlow.

  3. 03
    Soft, unhurried curves

    Rounded and even in weight, matching the calm, humanist shapes of Open Sauce Sans.

  4. 04
    Quiet luxury

    One confident shape with no ornament, at home on a €40 guide and a €15,000 trip alike.
