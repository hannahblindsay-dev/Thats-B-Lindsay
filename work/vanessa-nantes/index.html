The thinking.

(The fitting room and the sales pitch)
  1. 01One brand, two buyers.

    Affordable luxury, just above mainstream premium and just below high-end names. It had to sell directly to a time-poor professional woman and close partnerships with fashion agents and premium retailers across the Benelux and EU.

    The brief
  2. 02One consistent truth.

    Affordable luxury defined precisely enough to hold its ground, built around the capsule wardrobe: efficiency and timelessness as empowerment. For wholesale, the same idea became a brand that completes a retailer’s portfolio rather than competing inside it.

    The strategy
  3. 03Restraint, considered.

    Nocturne and Mangrove anchor the brand in poise, Verdant Clay adds tactile vitality, Honey Dune and Sunstone bring warmth against Ivory Silk. Timeless for headlines, Onest for body: premium on a shelf, credible in a wholesale deck.

    Creative direction
“This is fashion, redefined. Effortless, empowered, and enduring.”
Brand description · Vanessa Nantes styleguide

Where the brand landed.

(Window to woven tag)

From garment labels and woven tags to the boutique window, the identity carries the same restraint from the styleguide onto every touchpoint a customer or retailer actually sees.

Strength, carried lightly.

(The mark)

The elephant brandmark sits above the wordmark on the window, the bag and the woven tags: one quiet emblem for a brand built on restraint.

  1. 01
    The elephant

    Strength without aggression, and a memory that lasts: effortless strength, the first of the brand’s five values.

  2. 02
    The raised trunk

    The trunk curls up and back on itself, an old sign of good fortune and a small note of optimism in a calm mark.

  3. 03
    Mass and line

    A solid body cut through with fine lines for the ear and legs, so the detail reads as craftsmanship rather than decoration.

  4. 04
    The ground line

    The elephant stands on a single clean rule: grounded, composed and built to endure, like the capsule wardrobe it signs.

The takeaway

Two conversations, nothing diluted.

Vanessa Nantes needed a brand that could talk to the woman wearing it and the retailer selling it at the same time. That only works when the strategy comes first: define the gap precisely, then carry the same restraint into every room the brand walks into.
