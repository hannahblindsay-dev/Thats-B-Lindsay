The thinking.

(Depth over breadth)
  1. 01The brand had to be the proof.

    A boutique retention studio writing Klaviyo email and SMS flows for e-commerce. Every decision had to answer whether it looked like it was made by someone who actually knows what makes an inbox convert.

    The brief
  2. 02Depth over breadth.

    A category crowded with generalists treating email as one line item. Email Elixir was positioned as a specialist studio, with the site built as a trust engine: testimonials, low-friction call booking and a clear FAQ.

    The strategy
  3. 03Premium, a little magical.

    An animated logo and subtle motion, calibrated to feel premium without tipping into gimmick. Olivea’s handwritten display carries the human element, set against Haurora’s calm body copy.

    Creative direction
“It’s the human element in a world of automation.”

The elixir, bottled and carried through.

(Off screen)

The potion-bottle mark applied across the physical touchpoints that carry the brand off screen, from the studio’s own stationery to a client welcome gift.

A site built to be the first proof point.

Figma→Webflow+Animated logo→Calendly booking+NL / EN toggle

The identity was built out in Figma and developed in Webflow, with the animated logo, a Calendly-integrated booking flow and bilingual Dutch and English support via a translate toggle, backed by SEO, page speed, analytics and ad-pixel tracking.

A formula, sealed with care.

(The mark)

A potion bottle with an envelope at its heart: the whole promise of tailored emails and magical results, held in one small, crafted mark.

  1. 01
    The bottle

    A round potion flask: email treated as a formula, mixed carefully for each brand rather than poured from a template.

  2. 02
    The envelope inside

    At the heart of the bottle sits a letter. The email is the ingredient that makes the elixir work.

  3. 03
    The sparkle

    A four-point star just off the neck, the small spark of magic in “magical results”.

  4. 04
    The cork

    A hand-finished stopper in Cafe Noir: boutique, considered and sealed with care.

The takeaway

The brand was the first proof point.

Email Elixir couldn’t sell “tailored emails, magical results” from behind a brand that looked like everyone else’s. The identity and the site had to be evidence, before a single pitch, of a studio that understands exactly what makes an inbox convert.
