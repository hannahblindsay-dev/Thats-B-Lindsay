Trading without the noise.

Van Straaten Trading teaches skill that lasts to traders who’ve already lost money to hype. We built the anti-guru: a brand that stands out precisely by refusing to perform.

The thinking.

(A brand that refuses to perform)
  1. 01Building the anti-guru.

    The target trader had usually already lost money and time to mentors who oversold a lifestyle and hid the substance behind a paywall. The brand exists to teach skill that lasts: no hype, no signals, no shortcuts.

    The brief
  2. 02Value first, by design.

    The customer journey was mapped before any visual work started, from sceptical stranger to paying student. Free community, paid courses and mentorship became one coherent path rather than four competing offers.

    The strategy
  3. 03Steady, not loud.

    Foundation, a deep blue-black, anchors the brand as disciplined and unshaken by market noise, with Graphite Zone and a growth-green trio for skill-based progress. Lexend Deca: no decoration, only structure.

    Creative direction
“The market rewards patience, discipline and understanding, and so do we.”
Company description · Van Straaten Trading brand guidelines

Where the brand landed.

Webflow site+Video editing team→ManyChat automations→Free and paid content

The positioning carried through into a Webflow site, a content strategy connected directly to a video editing team, and ManyChat automations delivering free and paid content.

A letter that breaks out.

(The mark)

One heavy, quiet monogram that carries the brand on everything from caps to AirPods cases: structure first, no decoration.

  1. 01
    The N

    A single geometric N, built from two upright stems and one diagonal: the founder’s initial, reduced to pure structure.

  2. 02
    The arrow

    The top right corner resolves into an arrow pointing up and out, the direction every trader is working towards.

  3. 03
    The break

    The arrow sits just apart from the letter, like price clearing a level. Progress shown as a breakout earned, not a promise.

  4. 04
    The weight

    Heavy strokes and softened joints: steady, not loud, and built on the same calm geometry as Lexend Deca.

The takeaway

In a market built on noise, build something quieter.

The palette, the copy, the offer structure: all restraint, on purpose. That’s what makes the brand distinct in a category that usually shouts, and it only works because the strategy came before the screens.
